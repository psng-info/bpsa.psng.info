Privacy policy

We collect as little data as possible. This page explains what happens when you visit this website. The legally binding version is the German Datenschutzerklärung.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:
Lennert van de Kreeke
Tucholskystraße 7
10117 Berlin
Deutschland
Email: bpsa@psng.info

2. Hosting

This website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub automatically processes technical access data sent by your browser, including your IP address, the date and time of the request, and your browser and operating system. This data is used to deliver and secure the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). It may be transferred to the USA. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

3. Contact form

If you use our contact form, the data you enter (name, email address and message) is processed to handle your request, based on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). The form is forwarded to bpsa@psng.info via Formspree (Formspree Inc., USA), which receives your entries and your IP address. See Formspree's privacy policy for details. We do not share your data with anyone else and delete it once it is no longer needed for your request. You can also email us directly instead.

4. Fonts

All fonts on this website are served from our own server. No connection to third party servers (such as Google Fonts) is made.

5. Embedded videos

Recordings of our events are embedded as YouTube videos. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you actively start a video; the preview images are stored on our own server. Only when you click play is a player from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) loaded via youtube-nocookie.com, which transmits your IP address to the provider. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking the video (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR).

6. External links

We link to external platforms and services, including WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Forms (team sign-up) and Google Calendar. When you click these links, you leave our website, and the respective provider is responsible for processing your data under its own privacy policy. The calendar file (.ics) for Apple and Outlook is created directly in your browser without sending any data.

7. Analytics and local storage

This website uses GoatCounter for privacy friendly counting of page views and link clicks. GoatCounter sets no cookies and creates no user profiles; it only produces aggregated, non-personal statistics. Loading the counting script technically transmits your IP address to the provider, but it is not stored. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in simple audience measurement (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). See GoatCounter's privacy policy for details.

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This information never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data stored about you, to have it corrected, deleted or its processing restricted, to object to processing and to data portability. You can withdraw any consent at any time. To do so, contact bpsa@psng.info. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

9. Updates to this policy

We update this privacy policy when needed to reflect changes in the law or in our website. Last updated: November 2026.
